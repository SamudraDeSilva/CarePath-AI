# Phase 2 - Synthetic data engineering methodology

**Research-only simulation.** No NHS records, clinical effectiveness estimates, transport eligibility decisions or live communications are included. The dataset is invented and must not be represented as NHS evidence. All IDs are fictional. The original v0.1.1 database and notebook remain unchanged. The integrated v0.2.0 database contains the Phase 1 tables and fixtures plus three research tables. `schema_version=1` remains the foundation compatibility version; `research_runs` identifies the extension and seed.

## Population and timing
- 12,500 adults, 2-6 appointments per patient (2,500 patients in each count), 50,000 total. 156 synthetic calendar weeks from 2 January 2023. Each patient has at most one appointment per week on a fixed weekday. Appointment and index timestamps are UTC, simplified (no daylight-saving adjustments). Age, deprivation proxy, distance, employment, vehicle access and channel preferences are synthetic baseline attributes held constant within patient. No actual postcodes or demographic group membership inferred.
- Appointment booked 7-90 days before visit. Survey invitation/response snapshot at T−3 days. Cancellation/rescheduling may occur before the T−2 day prediction/offer index. Attendance/no-show occurs at visit. Early dispositions are not treated as no-shows and are excluded from the binary cohort; rescheduled visits are not linked to replacement bookings. Previous outcomes are counted only when the scheduled visit time precedes the current index. Historical no-show rate denominator includes all previous appointments, including cancelled and rescheduled ones.
- Non-response simulated conditional on recorded preferences and fictional patient factors. Missing survey answers remain null, not zero. No survey is generated in the operational survey table for non-responders. Actual survey submission is optional, uses a separate fictional demonstration appointment, preserves unanswered fields and records support consent without triggering external actions.

## Predictive dataset and evaluation
- Prediction question: among appointments still active at T−2 days, estimate observed non-attendance under the historical simulated offer policy. This is not an untreated counterfactual risk model. For Phase 3, compare the same cohort and splits with the baseline feature allowlist and survey-enhanced allowlist. Never use treatment, outcome, oracle data, patient/appointment IDs or post-index data as predictors.
- Split by index date: train before 2025-01-04; validation from 2025-01-04 through 2025-07-04; test from 2025-07-05. The cut dates fall between weekly appointment/index cycles, ensuring labels in one period are observed before the next period's index. Repeated patients can appear in different periods (appropriate for prospective evaluation of known patients); report this overlap and separately assess cold-start/generalisation in Phase 3. A patient's prior outcomes may legitimately inform subsequent visits. No future outcomes in features.
- Numeric median imputation with missingness indicators and scaling; categorical constant imputation and one-hot encoding, unseen categories ignored. Both preprocessors fitted on training only. For cross-validation refit within each fold; saved preprocessors are for final chronological evaluation only. No oversampling or trained predictive model in Phase 2.
- Age and deprivation proxy are synthetic features subject to future fairness and appropriateness review. Do not use the system to deny access to care.

## Causal simulation
Four mutually exclusive hypothetical *offers*: usual care (no additional offer), reminder, transport support, scheduling support. These are purely research exposures, not actual service delivery or bookings; they do not imply SMS consent, transport eligibility or staff approval. Conditional assignment uses a softmax of observed covariates with a uniform 12% exploration component (minimum probability 3% per arm); random assignment conditional on recorded covariates supports overlap by construction. The outcome is sampled from a known logistic baseline with simulated heterogeneous risk reductions per arm. Transport offer benefit depends on underlying transport/financial difficulty; scheduling benefit depends on timing/caring barriers. A patient-specific latent effect influences outcomes but not assignment after conditioning on the recorded assignment covariates. All potential risks and known assignment probabilities are written to the **separate** simulation oracle. Oracle is for evaluation only, never model training or feature selection. For Phase 5 estimate effects from observed exposures and outcomes first, then benchmark against oracle risk differences. Treatment estimates are not causal evidence outside this simulator. Observed arm rates are descriptive, not causal effects. No combined intervention arm is modeled yet. 

## File inventory
- `data/appointments.csv`: 50,000 research appointments including observed history, survey responses, offer and outcome.
- `data/patient_surveys.csv`: historical survey responders only; `data/patients.csv`: unique baseline patient demographics and fictional preferences.
- `data/interventions.csv`: active-cohort historical hypothetical offers and observed outcomes (join to appointments using appointment_id).
- `data/causal_oracle.csv`: separate ground truth, never predictors. Contains rows for inactive visits with null probabilities.
- `data/splits/{train,validation,test}.csv`: survey-enhanced raw feature tables plus identifiers and target; baseline selection using `config/phase2_features.json`. The identifier columns are for joining/audit only.
- `models/phase2_*_preprocessor.joblib`: local generated scikit-learn transformers, not prediction models. Only load trusted joblib files. Refit if environment versions differ.
- `reports/phase2/`: checks, actual test output, summaries, plots and simulated treatment effects.
- `database/carepath_ai_phase2.db`: integrated research database; original `database/carepath_ai.db` is retained as a Phase 1 snapshot. Historical exposures only in research_appointments; no live operational records generated from exposures.
