# v0.2.0 - Data Engineering and Patient Survey

## Overview

This release extends the CarePath-AI foundation (v0.1.1) with reproducible synthetic healthcare data generation, patient survey management and data engineering pipelines for subsequent predictive analytics research.

## Key developments

- 50,000 synthetic appointment records across 12,500 fictional patient profiles.
- 40,052 simulated historical survey responses and longitudinal attendance histories.
- Structured patient survey interface and persistent response management.
- Exploratory data analysis, feature engineering and missing data handling.
- Chronological training, validation and testing partitions, with preprocessing fitted on training data only.
- Simulated historical intervention assignments and separately stored causal simulation ground truth.
- Extension of the SQLite database architecture from 14 to 17 tables.
- Updated research methodology, data dictionary and reproducibility documentation.

## Reproducibility and limitations

The Phase 2 notebook (`notebooks/Phase_02_Data_Engineering.ipynb`) regenerates the synthetic datasets, preprocessing artefacts and integrated research database. Install the dependencies listed in `requirements.txt` before execution. The independent regression suite passed; the original notebook cells completed, although kernel connection and shutdown issues were reported. The optional graphical survey widget was not verified in the development environment; the editable survey and submission backend were tested.

All patient records, attendance outcomes and simulated intervention effects are synthetic. This release does not include trained predictive or causal estimators or live healthcare integrations. CarePath-AI is a research prototype and has not been clinically validated. It is not intended for live healthcare decision making.
